About

The Cosmic Sound Explorer

DJ Nova is an electronic music producer and DJ known for blending deep house, techno, and ambient sounds into a unique cosmic experience. With over a decade in the industry, Nova has performed at major festivals and underground clubs worldwide, creating immersive sonic journeys that transport audiences to other dimensions.

His productions have been featured on labels like Stellar Records and Cosmic Waves, earning support from industry heavyweights. Nova's sets are a carefully curated mix of driving rhythms, ethereal melodies, and unexpected sonic textures.
